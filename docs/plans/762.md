## 3. Questions settled

All settled with the maintainer on 2026-11-15, and written into the specs as Agreed:

1. **#763 reaches assignments.** A phase cable change fades everything its module drives: inputs that fade by type, Audio Out, and assignments whose source is that module. The walk follows phase-typed cables downstream.
2. **Undo inside a stack** fades per copy, as outside a stack, in all four directions.
3. **Status wording:** "Agreed on the plan for #762 (2026-11-15)".
4. **#762: fade the setting.** An on/off, depth, or polarity change blends the old setting into the new one over 5 ms while the source plays. A removed assignment still holds its last value and ramps, because its source is gone. Both are click-free and end on exactly nothing; with an audio-rate source they differ slightly during the fade.
5. **#768: removals never fail for room.** Remove part, undo of a swap-in, and redo of Remove part fall back to rule 9's 5 ms ramp per copy when the fades do not fit the module limit, inside and outside a stack. A swap that adds a part stays refused whole, with a message that says why and what to do.
6. **#763: a Unit Delay on a phase path** is an accepted known limit (a short sweep through the wave), listed in "What still steps".
7. **#763: inserting a Phase Shift at offset 0** into a running oscillator makes a 5 ms dip. Accepted; named in the Cost line. A follow-up issue covers a "no jump, no fade" check (filed during the build).

No open questions remain.

## 4. The design

The full design is in the specs:

- #762 and #763: `docs/design/live-patching.md`, "How rule 9 handles assignment changes and phase cable changes".
- #768: `docs/design/sub-patches.md`, "Empty-slot swaps inside a stack", and ADR-0046.

In short:

### #762: assignment changes fade the setting

- Each setting is a straight-line map of the source sample, `g·x + h` (`AssignmentRuntime::map()`; finite because depth is clamped).
- A new `ContributionRamp` holds `g0`, `h0`, and its position. It starts from a value (`g0 = 0, h0 = last`: a swap's fresh ramp) or from a setting (a settings change). `InputFade` and `ModSource::Leaving` keep `Ramp`.
- `ModSource::Live` gets a ramp slot on every continuous source, `last`, and `planned` (so `Schedule::fade_keys` lists only planned contributions).
- `UpdateAssignment` only writes settings and marks the runtime changed. Before the block, each changed runtime is compared with the settings its ramps head for (`played`), so A→B→A in one batch, or the same values twice, is bit-identical to no command. Nothing ramps before the engine's first block.
- Undo and redo: at a swap, `take_contributions_from` compares the old runtime's `played` with the new settings and starts a setting ramp if they differ. This covers undo's remove and restore with the same id, and stack copies restored by `sync_modes`. (Chosen over recording settings in `Wiring`, which can hold settings the engine no longer plays after a command.)
- The blend is written in the two-weight form with `hold_finite`; a source is not read while `g0` is 0, so a NaN source cannot reach `h`. Panic ends the ramps. A new copy or right side takes copy 0's or the left side's ramp state.

### #763: a phase replug fades downstream

- The walk runs in `FadePlan::new` (`crates/loom-core/src/compile/fades.rs`), after the two `fresh` loops and before `old_reads`, as a worklist.
- Seeds: inputs that carry a phase and changed cable. "Carries a phase" reads ports with `descriptor.inputs.get(port)`; through the editor's mixers it is what `Carried` finds upstream. Exact rewires (a slot swap's own) never seed it.
- Each input reached: fades by type → joins `fresh`, walk stops; carries a phase → walk goes on; anything else (gate, trigger, a Select's `member`, any input of an unknown module) → switches, walk stops.
- Assignments from a seed join `live` as fresh. `build.rs`, `cable_fade.rs`, and the engine do not change for #763.

### #768: empty-slot swaps inside a stack

- `fades_with_thru` (`crates/loom-patch/src/hierarchy/slots.rs`) drops its stack test; `add_copy_fades` loses its early return on `None`.
- Out of the slot: `CopyFade` records `S_k` and `D_k`, read before the sync. `fade_k.out → D_k` replaces `S_k → D_k` only while that cable is still there; with no `S_k`, only while `D_k`'s module exists and its input has no cable.
- A sub-patch part: `in_copy` finds the inner output module in each copy.
- Module limit: room is measured with the commit's own rule, never counted by hand. A new read, `Controller::slots_needed()`, gives the copy slots the next commit would take (the same `width::infer` and copy list as `commit_synced`). An edit fits when that is at most `free_module_slots()`, measured after the sync, because a fade's width follows its cables (Dynamics, with stereo ports, in an Effect slot on a mono signal in a stack of 4: the parts take 4 slots, the fades 8).
- A removal adds its fades, syncs, and measures. If it does not fit, `Hierarchy::drop_removal_fades` removes the fades (never sent, so their slots free at once) and the removal takes today's no-fade path: rule 9 ramps over 5 ms. A swap in that does not fit is refused and taken back, as a failed sync is today.
- Undo and redo: `undo::check` refuses a step whose slot is gone, and counts restored modules at the width recorded with the step (`ModuleState::slots`, through `Controller::slots_of`), leaving out modules under swapped slots through one helper, `undo::swapped_ids`, shared with `apply_fills`, `apply_removals`, and `apply_rest`. `App::step` measures after `apply_fills`, after `apply_removals`, and after `apply_rest`: a removal yields its fades first; if a fill still does not fit, the swaps are taken back and the step is refused with the history kept.
- ADR-0046 records why each copy's thru ends are kept at the swap. ADR-0044 stays Accepted and unedited.

- **#762** (`crates/loom-modules/tests/cable_fade.rs`, `crates/loom-app/src/undo/tests.rs`): a moving Sine source checked frame by frame against `old(x_k)·(1−t) + new(x_k)·t` within 1e-6; a drag of five depth changes; A→B→A in one batch and a command before the first block, both bit-identical; a restored assignment with new settings, and undo of a depth change, fade; a target that turns stereo mid-blend; a NaN source; panic; a burst of `UpdateAssignment` in `no_alloc.rs` and the modulation bench.
- **#763** (`cable_fade.rs`, `compile/fades/tests.rs`): a phase cable plugged, moved, and pulled on a Sine into Audio Out; Phase → Phase Shift → Sine; a gate downstream switches; an assignment sourced from the replugged module ramps; an unknown module downstream; a 4-voice phase bundle replugged to mono (fails if the walk runs after `old_reads`); `no_alloc`. ## 9. For the pull request description

- #763: the spec differs from the issue's idea (fade the module's output) and wins: it fades the consumers' inputs, follows phase cables, and includes assignments.
- #768: the issue asks to edit ADR-0044's note; an Accepted ADR is never edited, so ADR-0046 refines it.
- Behaviour changes to list:
  - Removals at the module limit now succeed with a 5 ms ramp, where Remove part was refused outside a stack.
  - An undo or redo at the module limit used to pass `undo::check`, fail in `add_fades`, and clear the history. Now it is refused, either by `undo::check` before anything changes or by the room measure after a sync, and what it changed is taken back with the history kept. Two known limits of the take back ([sub-patches](../design/sub-patches.md#empty-slot-swaps-inside-a-stack-agreed-on-the-plan-for-762-2026-11-15), Undo and redo): a parameter value sent to a running module before the commit is sent again with its old value, and the stack copies of a module the step deletes come back from reset.
  - Every refusal at the limit (a swap into an empty slot, a swap between two parts, a refused undo or redo) has one new text that says why and what to do.
  - `contribution_of` reads `last`, which fixes two existing gaps: it used the newest settings for the last frame, and it gave 0 for a silent source whose ramp was still running.
- Manual checks, not done yet:
  - [ ] #762: listen to an assignment turned off and on, a depth change, and a polarity flip on a running patch; no click.
  - [ ] #762: drag the depth of an assignment from an audio-rate source; it glides with no flutter.
  - [ ] #762: hold an arrow key on the inspector's depth field; the steps glide with no click.
  - [ ] #763: listen to a phase cable plugged, moved, and pulled on a running oscillator, directly and through a Phase Shift; no click.
  - [ ] #768: listen to Remove part, a swap into the empty slot, and the undo and redo of each, inside a stack; every copy crossfades.

Changing it touches rule 9's mixer rule for every cable change, not only #763, so it is a follow-up candidate, not part of this batch.
